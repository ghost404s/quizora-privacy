Quizora Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 11, 2026
Last Updated: September 11, 2026

Quizora ("Quizora", "we", "us", or "our") is an AI-powered quiz and exam preparation application developed and operated by Phibit Studio.

This Privacy Policy explains how Quizora collects, uses, stores, and shares information when you use our mobile application and related services.

1. Information We Collect

Depending on how you use Quizora, we may collect or process different types of information.

1.1 Account Information

If Quizora provides account creation or sign-in features, we may collect information associated with your account, such as:

  • Email address
  • Display name or username
  • User ID
  • Authentication information provided through supported authentication services

1.2 Quiz and Study Content

Quizora allows users to generate quizzes from educational materials. Users may provide:

  • PDF documents
  • Images
  • Text
  • Questions and answers
  • Quiz titles and subjects
  • Exam results and scores

This information may be processed to generate quizzes, provide exam functionality, calculate results, and improve your learning experience.

1.3 PDFs and Images

If you choose to upload a PDF or image to Quizora, its content may be processed by AI services to extract educational information and generate quiz questions.

You are responsible for ensuring that you have the necessary rights or permission to upload and process any document, image, or other content.

2. How We Use Information

We may use information to:

  • Provide and operate Quizora
  • Generate quizzes using AI
  • Process PDFs, images, and text
  • Allow users to take quizzes and exams
  • Calculate scores and display results
  • Provide premium features
  • Process subscriptions and purchases through Google Play
  • Display advertisements
  • Analyze app usage and performance
  • Detect and prevent abuse or fraudulent activity
  • Diagnose crashes and technical problems
  • Improve Quizora and its features

3. AI Quiz Generation

Quizora may use third-party artificial intelligence services to process content submitted by users and generate quiz questions.

When you request AI quiz generation, information such as text extracted from an uploaded PDF, image, or other submitted content may be sent to an AI service for processing.

AI-generated content may contain mistakes or inaccuracies. Quizora does not guarantee that every AI-generated question, answer, explanation, or result is accurate.

4. Firebase Services

Quizora may use Google Firebase services, including Firebase Analytics, Firebase Crashlytics, Firebase Authentication, Firebase Realtime Database, or other Firebase services depending on the features enabled in the application.

Firebase may process information such as app usage information, device information, crash information, authentication information, and information necessary to provide synchronization or other Firebase-powered features.

5. Analytics

Quizora may use Firebase Analytics to understand how users interact with the application.

Analytics information may include screens or features viewed, app interactions, session information, device and app information, and general usage statistics.

6. Advertising and AdMob

Quizora may display advertisements using Google AdMob.

AdMob and its partners may collect or process information such as advertising identifiers, device information, IP address, approximate location, ad interaction information, and app usage information for advertising and measurement purposes.

7. Google Play Billing and Subscriptions

Quizora may offer optional premium features through subscriptions purchased through Google Play Billing.

Payments are processed by Google Play. Quizora does not directly receive or store your complete credit card, debit card, or other payment-card information.

Quizora may receive purchase-related information such as subscription status, product information, transaction identifiers, or purchase tokens necessary to provide premium features and verify purchases.

8. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information as a standalone product.

We may share or allow access to information with service providers when necessary to operate Quizora.

These may include:

  • Google Firebase
  • Google AdMob
  • Google Play services
  • AI service providers used for quiz generation
  • Analytics and crash-reporting providers
  • Other technology providers necessary to operate Quizora

9. Data Storage and Security

Depending on the features you use, information may be stored locally on your device or on servers operated by Quizora or third-party service providers.

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures designed to protect information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction.

However, no method of electronic storage or transmission over the Internet can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

10. Children's Privacy

Quizora is intended for general educational use.

Quizora does not knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable laws.
